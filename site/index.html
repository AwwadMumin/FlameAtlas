WHY MICROGRAVITY MATTERS

Different gravity.
Different flame behavior.

In microgravity, buoyancy-driven airflow is greatly reduced, and a candle flame becomes a sphere. NASA has no combustion data yet at Moon or Mars gravity, so Flame Atlas shows the microgravity and Earth bounds instead of guessing.
